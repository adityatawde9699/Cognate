# Cognate v3.1.0-rc.6

Production-roadmap candidate for R20, the core R21 planner, and local R22 team workflows. This is a prerelease; production acceptance remains incomplete.

## Changes

- Saved plans retain placement and overflow explanations across reloads. Plan status detects changed tasks, calendars and working hours, legacy records and unreadable details. Capture/review actions and persistent errors support recovery after failed planning.
- Keyboard rescheduling uses labelled time/duration inputs, focus trapping/restoration and atomic pin/reflow. Planner controls announce pin state. Narrow layouts keep capture, short task blocks, the editor and backlog reachable.
- Team availability is opt-in, signed by the member, title-free and expires after 15 minutes. Unknown, expired and read-only capacity is excluded explicitly; busy overlaps count once. Tasks assigned to departed members remain unresolved for review.
- Assignment proposals show task/recipient details and bind the device identity, date, share epoch, tasks, roster and planning inputs. One guarded history transaction applies all reviewed assignments; storage failures and stale or edited proposals commit nothing.
- Invite acceptance distinguishes a saved local invitation from pending initial sync. Manual assignments enforce project scope and current owner/editor permissions.

## Before upgrading

Export a backup and retain your recovery kit. Existing saved plans appear as needing review until Auto-plan creates a record with freshness inputs. Availability is shared only after choosing “Share my busy times”; older clients do not provide the new availability contract. Team schedules are previews, not calendar reservations.

The v2 sync journal requires a compatible relay. Share rotation requires new invitations; offline edits across rotation still need a transfer policy. This candidate leaves the stable automatic-update channel unchanged. Install it explicitly for evaluation. Updater artifacts are signed; OS signing/notarization remains outside this release configuration.
