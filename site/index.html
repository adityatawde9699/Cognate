09:00

09:00 — 11:00 deep work · the planner engine

A solver, not a suggestion.

Most planners give you an empty grid and wish you luck. Cognate treats your day as a constraint problem and solves it deterministically — same inputs, same plan, explainable to the minute. No model in the loop, nothing to hallucinate your Tuesday.

planner.rs
The scheduler is native Rust, mirrored in TypeScript for the web build. It places every task by priority, deadline, and estimated effort.
energy_curve
Cognate learns when you actually focus from your completion and pomodoro history, and gives your hardest work to your best hours.
calendar.ics
Import an .ics feed from Google, Outlook, Apple Calendar, or another provider. Meetings become hard walls and the plan flows around them.
auto_reflow
A meeting lands, a task slips, you drag a block — the day re-solves instantly and tells you what moved and why.
pinned
Some things are sacred. Pin a block and the solver plans everything else around it.

calendar status — .ics import works everywhere. Direct Google and Outlook free/busy sync is currently desktop-only and requires your own OAuth client ID; the browser and PWA use the .ics path.

14:00 — 15:30 sync · every device, zero trust

The relay can't read your week.

Every change is an entry in a CRDT operation log, sealed with a passphrase that exists only on your devices. Phones, laptops, offline edits — everything converges without conflicts. The relay in the middle is a few hundred lines of Rust, stores only ciphertext, and you can host it yourself.

16:00 — 17:00 collaboration · share a project, not your workspace

An invite is a key, not an account.

Sharing a project hands a teammate the key to that project's encrypted log — nothing else. Roles are enforced by cryptographic signatures, not server rules: revoke an editor and their future edits simply stop verifying. Comments, assignees, and presence ride the same log. Then the moat: the solver plans the whole team — it balances unassigned work into each member's real hours.

19:00 — 19:30 advisory hours · ai on your terms

The AI advises. It never decides.

Type a thought and a deterministic parser — no network, no model — schedules it. AI is optional garnish on top: bring your own API key, or click once and run a local model through Ollama. There is no hosted tier, because there is nothing of yours on anyone's server to charge for.
