Your day. Already planned.

Cognate takes a pile of tasks and calendar busy time, then builds a time-blocked day. Planning runs locally and deterministically — in Rust on desktop and a TypeScript mirror on the web. AI can help with estimates, but the solver places the work. Your data stays local unless you choose sync or a cloud AI provider.

09:00 — 11:00 deep work · the planner engine

A solver, not a suggestion.

Most planners give you an empty grid and wish you luck. Cognate treats your day as a constraint problem and solves it deterministically — matching inputs produce a repeatable result. AI does not choose time slots. You can inspect the scheduling rationale and re-plan when your tasks or availability change.

planner.rs
The scheduler is native Rust, mirrored in TypeScript for the web build. It places every task by priority, deadline, and estimated effort.
energy_curve
An optional focus-time heuristic uses completion and Pomodoro history to estimate when demanding work may fit best.
calendar.ics
Import .ics busy time from Google, Outlook, Apple Calendar, or another provider. Planner availability accounts for imported events.
re_plan
Change tasks, pin a block, or update availability, then run Auto-plan to rebuild the day. Background reflow checks about every five minutes while the app is open.
pinned
Some things are sacred. Pin a block and the solver plans everything else around it.

calendar status — .ics import is available across desktop and browser. Google and Microsoft free/busy connectors are an early desktop integration and need provider setup; verify freshness in the app before relying on them.

13:15 — 13:30 desktop companion · reminders while tucked away

Close the window. Keep your reminders.

On desktop, closing the window hides Cognate in the system tray. While the app is still running, it checks your local plan and sends native reminders for scheduled block starts, tasks due today, and overdue tasks.

Your plan can tap you on the shoulder.

Reminders use the local SQLite database. Turn on desktop notifications in Cognate and allow them in your operating system. Reopen from the tray, or choose Quit when you want to stop the app.

Reminders pause while your computer sleeps and stop after you quit Cognate.

14:00 — 15:30 sync preview · optional encrypted relay

Your relay stores encrypted snapshots.

Cognate includes an optional passphrase-encrypted sync path and a small Rust relay that stores sealed data. Sync is still being hardened: operation coverage, conflict recovery, and freshness need more work before promising seamless, conflict-free multi-device use. The app remains useful on one device without a relay.
